<section class="section">
  <h2>What this Design Patterns section includes</h2>
  <p>The design patterns material in this repository focuses on the patterns that matter most in real Java backend work. Interviewers test whether you understand when and why to use Singleton, Factory, Builder, Strategy, Observer, and Decorator — not just their definitions, but their trade-offs and production implications.</p>
  <p>A strong preparation flow means knowing how patterns interact with Spring's DI container, how Proxy powers AOP and lazy-loading, and when a pattern introduces more complexity than it solves.</p>

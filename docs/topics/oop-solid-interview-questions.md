<section class="section">
  <h2>What this OOP and SOLID section includes</h2>
  <p>Object-oriented programming and SOLID principles are the foundation of Java development. Interviewers test whether candidates truly understand why these principles exist, not just their definitions. The questions cover the four pillars of OOP, each SOLID principle with practical examples, and the trade-offs of applying these principles in real codebases.</p>
  <p>A strong preparation approach covers how SOLID connects to testability, how Interface Segregation relates to microservice contracts, and when strict adherence to SOLID principles can over-engineer a solution.</p>
</section>

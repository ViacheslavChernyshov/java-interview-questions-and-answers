## 🎯 Recommended Study Tracks

### 🟢 Junior Level Track (Core Fundamentals)
1. **DI & Injection:** Q01, Q02, Q03 (Inversion of Control, constructor vs field injection).
2. **Bean Basics:** Q04, Q05 (`@Component`, `@Bean`, `ApplicationContext`).
3. **Scopes & Stereotypes:** Q10, Q12, Q25, Q26 (Singleton vs Prototype, `@Service`, `@Repository`, `@Autowired`).
4. **Spring Boot Starters:** Q20, Q22 (Autoconfiguration concepts, starter BOMs).

### 🟡 Middle Level Track (Internals & Production Mechanics)
1. **Lifecycle & Hooks:** Q06, Q07, Q08, Q09 (`BeanPostProcessor`, `@PostConstruct`, `@PreDestroy`).
2. **Web Scopes:** Q11 (`request`, `session`, `application` scoped proxies).
3. **Proxy Engine:** Q13, Q14 (CGLIB subclassing vs JDK dynamic interface proxies).
4. **Transactions & Self-Invocation:** Q17, Q18, Q19 (`@Transactional` rollback rules, proxy bypassing, 4 solutions).
5. **Boot Composition:** Q21, Q27, Q28, Q29 (`@SpringBootApplication`, `@Primary`, `@Qualifier`, `@Profile`).

### 🔴 Senior Level Track (Architecture & Deep Internals)
1. **AOP Weaving & Interception:** Q15, Q16 (Spring AOP runtime proxying vs AspectJ compile-time weaving).
2. **Autoconfiguration Engine:** Q20, Q23 (`@ConditionalOn*`, `AutoConfiguration.imports` vs `spring.factories`).
3. **Configuration Modes:** Q24 (`@Configuration(proxyBeanMethods = true)` Full CGLIB mode vs Lite mode).
4. **Circular Dependency Engine:** Q07 (3-level cache: `singletonObjects`, `earlySingletonObjects`, `singletonFactories`).
